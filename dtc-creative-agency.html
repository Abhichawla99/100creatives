DTC creative agency, branding agency or marketing agency: which one do you need?

By Abhi Chawla, founder · Last updated: 2026-10-28

"DTC agency" is a label for three different businesses: a branding agency that decides what the brand is and how it looks, a marketing agency that runs paid media and growth, and a creative agency that makes the ads and product imagery those channels run. Hire the one that matches the job that is failing today. 100 Creatives is the third kind, an AI-powered creative agency in Calgary that makes static and video ads for Meta, TikTok and Google, quoted in writing from $4,000.

Three businesses called a DTC agency

Search "DTC agency" and the results mix studios that design packaging, firms that manage seven-figure Meta budgets and shops that produce ads. They all sell to direct-to-consumer brands, and they all say "growth". They do different work, bill in different ways and fail in different ways, so comparing their prices side by side tells you very little until you know which type each one is.

A brand usually needs all three over its life, in roughly that order. The mistake that costs the most is hiring the wrong one for today's problem: paying a branding agency to fix a Meta account, or paying a media buyer to squeeze results out of the same four ads.

What does a DTC branding agency do?

A DTC branding agency answers "who is this brand, and how does it look?" The work ends in a brand book: the positioning line, the logo and its rules, typefaces, the color palette with exact values, packaging, photography direction and tone of voice. Many also design and build the Shopify store, because for a direct-to-consumer brand the site is the shelf.

You need one before launch, when a product is ready and nothing else is, and again for a rebrand, when the current identity is blocking growth or the brand is moving up in price. You don't need one because this quarter's ads underperformed. A branding agency rarely makes the dozens of ad variations a paid social account uses in a month, and the ones that do usually price them as a separate engagement.

Branding studios mostly quote per project, and few publish a number. One that does: Darkroom lists its Brand Studio from $12,500 and website development from $60,000, without saying whether those are one-off or monthly fees (Darkroom pricing, checked October 2026). Ask for a fixed fee, a list of deliverables and the number of revision rounds before you sign. Then ask who owns the source files. The brand book and the working files (not only the exported PDFs) are what every later vendor, including your creative agency, will build from.

What does a DTC marketing agency do?

A DTC marketing agency, often called a growth or performance marketing agency, runs the paid channels. It sets budgets, structures campaigns, launches tests, watches cost per purchase and reports on results. Some add email and SMS, landing pages and retention. The media buyer is the core of it: the person deciding where the next dollar of ad spend goes.

Prices are usually monthly. On its pricing page Darkroom listed paid media management from about $5,000 a month, and a separate performance creative service from about $8,000 a month (Darkroom pricing, checked October 2026). That split is common: the agency running your ads isn't necessarily the one making them. Bought together from one agency at those list prices, media management and creative start at $13,000 a month before any ad spend.

How a DTC marketing agency charges

Marketing agencies bill in one of three ways. Darkroom, which sells this service, describes all three in its own guide to agency costs, so read them as one seller's view of the market (Darkroom, marketing agency cost 2026, checked October 2026):

HybridA base fee plus a lower percentage above a spend threshold$8,000 a month plus 8% of spend above $50,000Where the threshold sits, and whether creative is inside the base

The same guide says most agencies charge 10% to 20% of ad spend or a flat fee, whichever is greater. Run that against your own budget before the first call. At $30,000 a month in Meta spend, 10% to 20% comes to $3,000 to $6,000, so a $5,000 flat floor is what you would pay. At $100,000 a month the percentage wins and the fee becomes $10,000 to $20,000. Below $25,000 a month in spend, a $5,000 floor is more than 20% of your media budget, which is the point to price a freelance media buyer as well.

What to check in a DTC marketing agency proposal

Four lines in the proposal decide whether it works for you. First, how many new ads a month the fee includes, who makes them, and what happens when the account needs more; Darkroom prices creative as its own line, and many agencies do the same. Second, which channels are in scope: Meta and Google are the usual pair, while TikTok Shop, Amazon and retention email are often separate fees. Third, who owns the ad accounts and the pixel. They should sit in your business manager, with the agency added as a partner, so you keep the history if you leave. Fourth, the notice period and any minimum term.

A related question shows up in our Search Console data word for word: "when should a DTC ecommerce brand hire a Google Ads agency instead of a freelancer?" Our answer is that a freelance media buyer suits one channel and a clear testing plan. An agency starts to make sense once you run Google and Meta together, need cover when one person is away, or want strategy, reporting and creative handled as one account. What is DTC media?

When a DTC brand talks about media, it means paid media: the ad spend on Meta, TikTok, Google and, for larger brands, streaming TV, and the people who decide where that spend goes. A DTC media agency or media buyer plans budgets, builds campaigns, reads the results and moves money toward the ads that sell. The ads themselves usually come from somewhere else, which is why "DTC media" and "DTC creative" are separate searches and separate lines on an invoice.

Publishing uses the same term for something else: media companies that sell to their readers directly, through subscriptions and products, instead of renting an audience from the platforms. The Rebooting newsletter used it that way in "The rise of DTC media", with Morning Brew and Barstool among its examples (checked October 2026). The rest of this section is about buying ads.

What a DTC media buyer needs from creative

A media buyer can only choose among the ads in the account. Meta built Andromeda, its ad retrieval system, "to scale up to a large volume of ads creatives", and its engineers reported that more than a million advertisers used Meta's generative AI tools to make more than 15 million ads in one month (Engineering at Meta, December 2024, checked October 2026). Meta publishes no recommended number of ads. A Meta spokesperson told Marketing Brew in September 2026 that diversifying creative is a "longstanding best practice". A paid media director quoted in the same story said Meta treats near-identical ads, such as one video with different text overlays, as the same creative. Meta did not confirm how its ranking models do this (Marketing Brew, September 2026, checked October 2026).

So a good buyer asks for ads that change the idea: a new hook, a new offer, a different person holding the product, a static beside a video. A brief that says "five more like the winner" puts five copies of one ad into the account. What a DTC creative agency does

A DTC creative agency makes the ads. That means statics for feed, stories and display sizes, video ads for Reels and TikTok, UGC-style ads, and the product and lifestyle imagery all of those need. New ads are what a media buyer tests, and a brand spending steadily on Meta runs through them fast. We work out how many ads a given budget supports in how many ad creatives do I need.

Creative agencies sell in three shapes, and the shape matters as much as the price:

  • Performance creative agencies sell strategy plus production on a monthly fee. Creative Milkshake lists subscriptions from about $7,000 a month, says typical subscriptions run $7,000 to $50,000 a month, and quotes 3 to 7 weeks to delivery depending on scope (Creative Milkshake pricing, checked October 2026). The order to hire them in

Start from what is failing today. Four situations cover most DTC brands.

You have a product and no brand

Hire the branding agency first. Ads made without a brand book drift from one batch to the next, and you'll pay to remake them after the rebrand. A good brand book is also the most useful thing you can hand a creative agency later.

You have a brand, and ad spend is being wasted

Signs: campaigns restructured every week, no one can explain last month's cost per purchase, or the founder is buying media at midnight. Hire a media buyer or a marketing agency. More ads won't fix an account nobody is managing.

The account is run well, and results slide anyway

Signs: the same handful of ads has run for months, new tests stall because nothing new is ready, and your media buyer keeps asking for "more creative". This is the creative agency's job. Size the need first; if the gap is a steady stream of small requests, a subscription fits, and if it is a set of statics and videos for a launch or a season, a quoted batch fits.

You're moving the work in-house

Hire designers and keep a partner for overflow, or have an agency build the production pipeline your team runs. 100 Creatives builds those brand-trained pipelines, called Workflows & Engines, from $4,000 per build. We describe the handover in taking AI creative in-house.

Six questions to ask any DTC agency before you sign

Which of the three are you? An agency that says "all of them" should show you who does each part and how it is priced.

What exactly does the fee buy? A number of ads, a number of active requests, hours, or a share of ad spend. "Unlimited" plans are limited by active requests and turnaround; we worked through the math in unlimited ad creatives.

Who writes the briefs? Subscriptions usually expect you to. Performance creative agencies and quoted batches usually include it.

How do I leave? Notice periods, annual terms and minimums vary a lot between the pricing pages linked above. Read them before the first invoice.

Who owns the files? Get the layered source files, not only exports, so the next vendor or your own team can reuse them.

Is the portfolio client work or spec work? Plenty of agencies, us included, can't show client work because of NDAs. That is normal. Labeling spec work as spec work is the minimum you should expect. Frequently asked questions

What is a DTC agency?
It is any agency that sells to direct-to-consumer brands, and the label covers three different businesses. A DTC branding agency builds the name, identity, packaging and site. A DTC marketing agency runs paid media, email and growth. A DTC creative agency makes the ads and product imagery those channels run. Ask which of the three you are talking to before you compare prices, because they are not substitutes.
Should a DTC brand hire a marketing agency or a creative agency first?
Hire for the job that is failing. If your ads are fresh but spend is mismanaged, targeting is a mess or nobody reads the numbers, hire a media buyer or marketing agency. If the account is run well but results slide because the same few ads have been running for months, hire creative production. A founder still testing whether the product sells can often run a few ads in-house before paying either one.
