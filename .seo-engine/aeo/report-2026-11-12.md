## Problems found on our own site

1. **100creatives.vercel.app serves a full copy of the site** (HTTP 200, no redirect). Claude's search cited it and quoted an old cached version of /cpg-creative-agency saying we "shipped for Zero Lush, Barefoot Wines and Hiyo". It then flagged that as a contradiction with the spec-work label. The canonical tag points to www, but AI search indexes don't all respect canonicals. Fix: a host-based 308 redirect from the vercel.app host to www in vercel.json.
2. **Correction notes restate the old claim.** /dtc-creative-agency and /cpg-creative-agency say "an earlier version of this page described a full-service agency... a named senior team on a flat monthly retainer". Claude lifted "named senior team, flat retainer" as our current offer. Fix: cut the old claim from those notes, or cut the notes.
3. **The apex still redirects with 307, not 308** (OFFSITE item 1).
4. **The daily SEO engine last published on 2026-11-02.** The local checkout is still at the 2026-09-16 commit, with unstaged edits.
