How pricing works

The figures above are starting prices. Final project cost depends on scope — the number of SKUs or assets, the complexity of styling and retouching, turnaround requirements, and whether the engagement is one-off or ongoing.

You will receive a written quote confirming the full scope and total fee before any work begins and before any payment is taken. No work starts, and no charge is made, until you approve that quote in writing.

Payment terms

  • Quotes are issued in USD and are valid for 30 days.
  • Projects are invoiced in advance unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Accepted methods: card payment and bank transfer.
  • All fees are non-refundable once work has commenced — see our Refund Policy.
