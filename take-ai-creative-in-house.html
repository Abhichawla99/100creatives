What to ask for

Ask for the recipe

The final images are only part of what you need. Ask your agency for:

  • The reference set for each product, the same files the agency gave the model.
  • Every approved character sheet, and the terms for reusing each face.
  • The brand rules as written: hex codes, light, lens, grain, styling and the never list.
  • The prompt, model and references behind each kept photo. Overs exports a whole campaign with that record for every photo.
  • The rejects and the reasons. They tell your team what not to try again.
  • A clean version of every photo, before any type.

Put the list in the contract before work starts, along with who owns what. Ownership of AI-generated images depends on the law where you are and on each tool's terms, so ask counsel about your market.

What teams miss

What in-house teams underestimate

  • Review time. In our four hand-built campaigns it took 361 renders to get 45 keepers. At about 8 tries per keeper, 30 photos a month means looking at about 240 renders.
  • Model changes. The models move under you. OpenAI released GPT Image 2.5 on September 8, 2026 (OpenAI API changelog), and Google has scheduled the original Nano Banana model, Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, to shut down on October 2, 2026 (Gemini API pricing). A prompt that worked on one model may need rewriting for the next.
  • Spend. Model fees are small per photo, $0.27 to $1.69 per keeper at our average, but they add up on a card. With a bring-your-own-key tool, each person's key is their own bill.
  • Consistency across people. Two people prompting from memory will describe the same light differently. Written rules and a shared cast keep them on one look.
Test the handover

Test the handover with three photos

Before the agency moves on, pick three photos you approved and have your team remake them from the recipe alone, in the tool you'll use. If all three come back matching the originals within a few tries each, the handover works. If they don't, look for the missing piece (a reference, a rule or the character sheet) while the agency is still there to help find it.
