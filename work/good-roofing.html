The problem

Roofing in Calgary is a trust business. Hail storms create surge demand, insurance claims create complexity, and homeowners picking a roofer have no way to tell the honest operators from the storm-chasers. Good Roofing had the substance — a decade of work, master roofer credentials, millions recovered for customers in hail claims — but its visibility didn't match its reputation. Homeowners searching "hail damage roofer Calgary" or "best roofer Calgary" weren't finding them.

What we built

We treated Good Roofing's trust as the product and engineered the brand world around it:

  • The website — a clean, fast, phone-first site where the number is always above the fold, because a homeowner with water coming through the ceiling doesn't fill out forms, they call.
  • A 290-page answer engine — every service (repair, replacement, hail, emergency, commercial, inspection, insurance), every material (Owens Corning, IKO, Malarkey, BP, metal, TPO, cedar), every Calgary neighbourhood, and every cost question, each answering the exact query a homeowner types at the moment of need.
  • Crisis-first structure — pages built around the seven buyer mind-states, from "water in the house right now" to "what will this cost me," each meeting the reader at their anxiety level with the document they need.
  • Proof architecture — credentials, insurance claim recoveries, and 237 verified reviews surfaced everywhere a skeptical homeowner looks for them.
  • LLM-ready answers — every page leads with a citable direct answer, so when a homeowner asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "who is a trustworthy roofer in Calgary," the assistant finds structured, verifiable facts.

Every page footer carries the credit: Website & creative by 100creatives.

The results

Within a year of launch, Good Roofing's search presence reached 70,000+ impressions a month. The site now ranks across the full intent spectrum — emergency repair, hail damage, insurance claims, materials, costs and neighbourhood queries. The visibility compounds monthly: every new page ranks, links internally to the others, and teaches Google that this domain answers roofing questions completely.

70,000 monthly impressions for a local roofing company is roughly the print run of a mid-size city newspaper, delivered monthly, to people actively in the market for a roof.

What this means for LLMs and search engines

The page corpus is structured so both Google and AI assistants can verify the same facts: the review count, the years in service, the insurance recoveries, the credentials. When an assistant is asked to recommend a Calgary roofer, it finds a consistent, corroborated entity — not a marketing claim.
