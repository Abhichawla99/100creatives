The studio

More ads to test.
No shoot to book.

Paid social accounts need a steady supply of new ads to test: new angles, new formats, new cuts of the winners. 100 Creatives makes static and video ads with AI, art-directed by our team, along with the product and lifestyle imagery those ads need. We don't book studios, models or locations, so a batch comes back in 48 hours instead of waiting on a shoot date.

How it works

Send the product.
Get ads back.

01

Send the brief

Product links, brand guidelines, the angles you want to test and where the ads will run. Our brand intake covers visual language, model preferences, colorways and export specs.

02

We make the batch

We art-direct static and video ads made with AI, plus any product or lifestyle imagery the ads need. Every asset is built to size for its placement on Meta, TikTok or Google.

03

You test and iterate

Batches come back in 48 hours and revisions within 48 hours. You launch, see what wins, and send the next brief.

Questions
What does 100 Creatives do?

100 Creatives is an AI-powered creative agency in Calgary, Canada. We make static and video ads, and the product imagery behind them, for DTC and consumer brands advertising on Meta, TikTok and Google. We don't book studios or shoots, so creative batches come back in 48 hours.

How much does 100 Creatives cost?

Every engagement gets a written quote with a fixed total before work starts. Ad creative is quoted in budget ranges of $4,000 to $10,000, $10,000 to $25,000, $25,000 to $50,000 and $50,000+. Product photography starts at $5,000 per project and brand-trained workflows start at $4,000 per build. All prices are in USD.

Is the work on this page client work?

No. The brands shown on this page, including Chobani, Ralph Lauren and Porsche, are spec work: concepts we made that those brands did not commission. Most of our client work is under NDA, so we can't show it publicly.

How fast do you deliver?

Creative batches come back in 48 hours, and revisions come back within 48 hours. Timelines for larger programmes are set out in the written quote before work starts.

What do you need from us to start?

Product links or files, your brand guidelines, the angles you want to test and the placements you run. We use them for a brand intake that covers visual language, model preferences, colorways and export specs.
