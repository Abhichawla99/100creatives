100 Creatives is an AI-powered creative agency in Calgary, Canada, that designs static ads for Meta, TikTok and Google and makes the video ads and product imagery behind them. Every engagement gets a written quote with a fixed total, from $4,000, and creative batches come back in 48 hours.

Pick 100 Creatives if you run paid social and need a steady volume of static ads, with AI video and product imagery from the same team, quoted per engagement from $4,000.

  • Written quote with a fixed total, in ranges of $4,000 to $10,000, $10,000 to $25,000, $25,000 to $50,000 and $50,000+. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

Pricing

How the service is priced

Every engagement gets a written quote with a fixed total before work starts. Static and video ad creative is quoted in budget ranges of $4,000 to $10,000, $10,000 to $25,000, $25,000 to $50,000 and $50,000+, so the smallest engagement is $4,000. There's no unlimited plan and no published monthly subscription.

Quotes are valid for 30 days. Projects are invoiced in advance, and you can pay by card or bank transfer. Nothing is charged until you approve the quote in writing, and fees are non-refundable once work has started. Ongoing engagements can be stopped before the next billing date, and fees already paid are not refunded. Starting prices for our other services are on the pricing page.

Your options

Other ways to get static ads made

Freelancer

Fits a few ads with clear direction and a budget under $4,000. Each freelancer quotes per project, and you write the brief and manage the feedback.

In-house designer

Fits daily design work beyond ads, with a creative lead to direct it. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics puts median pay for graphic designers at $62,960 a year as of May 2025, before benefits.

Unlimited design subscription

Fits small requests every week, briefed by your team. ManyPixels starts at ~$699 a month and Kimp at ~$1,397, and output depends on active requests and turnaround.

Performance creative agency

Fits brands that want strategy and scripting included. Creative Milkshake starts at ~$7,000 a month and Darkroom's performance creative service at ~$8,000.
